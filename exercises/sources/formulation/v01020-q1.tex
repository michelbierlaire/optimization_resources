
An investment fund is trying to determine how to invest its assets for
the following year, in order to maximize its profit. Currently, the
fund has 2.5 million euros that it can invest in state bonds, real
estate loans, car loans or scholarship loans. The annual interest
rates of the listed investment types are $4\%$ for state bonds, $6\%$
for real estate loans, $8\%$ for car loans and $9\%$ for scholarship
loans.

To minimize risks, the investment fund allows only the selection of a strategy satisfying the following restrictions:
\begin{itemize}
	\item the amount invested in car and scholarship  loans must not exceed twice the amount invested in bonds;
	\item the amount invested in car loans must be larger or equal
          than the
          amount invested in  scholarship loans;
	\item the investment in car loans should not exceed the
          investment in real estate loans by more than $70\%$. 
\end{itemize}

Formulate this problem as an optimization problem by determining
\begin{enumerate}
	\item the decision variables,
	\item the objective function, and
	\item the constraint(s).
\end{enumerate}
