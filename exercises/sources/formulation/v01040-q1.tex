
Given the following optimization problem, transform it in such a
  way as to obtain a minimization problem in which all decision
  variables must be non negative and all constraints are defined by lower
  inequalities.
\[
\max -x_1^2+\sin x_2
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
6x_1-x_2^2 &\geq 1, \\
x_1^2+x_2^2 &=3, \\
x_1 &\geq 2, \\
x_2 & \in \mathbb{R}.
\end{align*}
