
 We apply a sequence of  simple transformations to
  the optimization problem.

\begin{enumerate}
\item A maximization problem whose objective function is $f(x)$ is equivalent to a minimization problem whose objective function is $-f(x)$:
$$\argmax_x f(x)=\argmin_x-f(x)$$
and
$$\max f(x)=-\min -f(x).$$
By applying statement (a) to our problem we obtain:

\[
-\min x_1^2-\sin x_2
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
6x_1 -x_2^2 & \geq 1,\\
x_1^2 +x_2^2 & = 3,\\
x_1 & \geq 2,\\
x_2 & \in \mathbb{R}.
\end{align*}

\item Now we transform the constraints.
  An equality constraint can be written as the combination of two inequalities.
$$g(x)=0 \Longleftrightarrow  \left\{ \begin{array}{l} g(x) \leq 0 \\
    g(x) \geq 0. \end{array} \right. $$

The optimization problem becomes 
\[
-\min x_1^2-\sin x_2
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
6x_1-x_2^2  & \geq 1\\
x_1^2+x_2^2 & \leq 3\\
x_1^2+x_2^2 & \geq 3\\
x_1 & \geq 2\\
x_2 & \geq 0.
\end{align*}


\item A constraint defined by a lower inequality can be multiplied by $-1$ to get an upper inequality:
$$g(x) \leq 0 \Longleftrightarrow  -g(x)\geq 0.$$
\
$$-\min x_1^2-\sin x_2$$
subject to
\begin{align*}
-6x_1 +x_2^2  & \leq -1\\
x_1^2+x_2^2 & \leq 3\\
-x_1^2-x_2^2 & \leq -3\\
x_1 & \geq 2\\
x_2 & \in \mathbb{R}.
\end{align*}


\item If a variable $x$ can take any real value, it can be replaced by two non negative artificial variables denoted by $x^+$ and $x^-$, such that
$$
x=  x^+-x^-.
$$
We can impose $x^+\geq 0$ and $x^- \geq 0$, without loss of
generality. We apply this to the variable $x_2$ in our formulation.\\

$$-\min x_1^2-\sin(x_2^+-x_2^-)$$
subject to
\begin{align*}
-6x_1 +(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2  & \leq -1\\
x_1^2 +(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2  & \leq 3\\
-x_1^2 -(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2  & \leq -3\\
x_1 & \geq 2\\
x_2^+ & \geq 0\\
x_2^- & \geq 0.
\end{align*}

\item In the presence of a constraint $x\geq a$, with $a \in \mathbb{R}$, a simple change of variable
$$
x=  \hat{x}+a$$
transforms the constraint in
$$\hat{x}\geq  0.$$
We apply this last principle to variable $x_1$, and we obtain:
$$
-\min(\hat{x}_1+2)^2- \sin(x_2^+-x_2^-)
$$
subject to
\begin{align*}
-6(\hat{x}_1+2)+(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2  & \leq -1\\
(\hat{x}_1+2)^2+(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2 & \leq 3\\
-(\hat{x}_1+2)^2-(x_2^+-x_2^-)^2 &\leq -3\\
\hat{x}_1 & \geq 0\\
x_2^+ & \geq 0\\
x_2^- & \geq 0.
\end{align*}
\end{enumerate}

As requested, it is a minimization problem, all decision variables are non negative and all
constraints are defined by lower inequalities. Any solution
$(\hat{x}_1,x_2^+,x_2^-)$ of this
problem corresponds to the following solution of the original problem:
\[
\begin{aligned}
  x_1 &= \hat{x}_1 + 2 \\
  x_2 &= x_2^+ - x_2^-.
\end{aligned}
\]
