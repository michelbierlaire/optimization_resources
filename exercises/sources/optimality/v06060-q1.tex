
 Consider the optimization problem $$\min -3x_1 - 4x_2$$ subject to
\begin{align*}
-x_1+x_2 & \leq 2\\
-3x_1+2x_2 & \geq -5\\
x_1+x_2 & \leq 4\\
x_1,x_2 & \geq 0
\end{align*}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Solve the problem by calculating the  value of the objective
  function at each vertex.
\item Write the dual of this problem.
\item Use the complementarity slackness conditions to solve the dual.
\end{enumerate}
