
Consider a quadratic function $$f=\frac{1}{2}x^TQx+g^Tx+c,$$
where $Q = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & \alpha\\ \alpha & 1
\end{pmatrix}$, $g\in \mathbb{R}^2$ and $c \in \mathbb{R}$.
\begin{enumerate}
\item For which values of $\alpha$ $f$ does not have a local minimum?
\item For which values of $\alpha$ $f$ has a unique global minimum?
\item What are the conditions on $g$ and $\alpha$ so that the problem has an infinite number of global minima?
\end{enumerate}
