
Consider the following linear problem:
\[
\min_{x \in \mathbb{R}^3} 2x_1+7x_2+3x_3
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
-2x_1-x_2+x_3 &\geq 1 \qquad  (y_1)\\
x_1+2x_2 &\geq 2 \qquad  (y_2)\\
 x_1,x_2,x_3 &\geq 0 .
\end{align*}
and its dual
\[
\max_{y \in \mathbb{R}^2} \quad y_1 + 2y_2
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
-2y_1+y_2 &\leq 2 \qquad  (x_1),\\
-y_1+2y_2 &\leq 7  \qquad  (x_2),\\
y_1 &\leq 3  \qquad  (x_3),\\
y_1,y_2&\geq 0.
\end{align*}	


\begin{enumerate}
\item  Solve the dual problem with the graphical method.
\item State the \textit{complementarity slackness} conditions for both primal and dual problems. Using these, determine the optimal solution to the primal problem. 
\item Verify the result that  claims that the optimal solution to the dual problem satisfies
\[
B^Ty^* = c_B,
\]
where $B$ is the basis matrix of the primal problem.
\end{enumerate}
