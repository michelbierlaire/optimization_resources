
Consider the following polyhedron represented in standard form:
\[
 \mathcal{P} = \{x \in \R^4 \mid Ax = b, x \geq 0\},
\]
with
\[
 A = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\  1 & -1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}, \qquad b = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix*}. 
\]

 Write the polyhedron in canonical form.
