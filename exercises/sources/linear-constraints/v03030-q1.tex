
 Consider the following linear optimization problem:
\begin{align*}
 & \min_{x \in \R} 2 x_1 + x_2 \\
 \text{subject to} & \\
 & x_1 + x_2 \geq 3, \\
 & x_1 + 2 x_2 \leq 6, \\
 & x_1 \geq 1, \\
 & x_2 \geq 1.
\end{align*}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Does the solution $x_1^* = 1$, $x_2^* = 2$ verify all the constraints of the problem? Which constraints are active at $(x_1^*, x_2^*)$?
    \item Answer the same questions with $x_1^* = 2$, $x_2^* = 1.5$.
    \item Answer the same questions with $x_1^* = 1$, $x_2^* = 1$.
\end{enumerate}
