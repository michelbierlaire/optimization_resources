
    First, the polyhedron needs to be written in standard form. In
    order to do so, we need to transform the constraints into equality
    constraints by introducing the slack variables $x_3$ and $x_4$ for
    the first and second equation, respectively. The constraints are
    then written as follows:
    \begin{align*}
        -x_1 + x_2 + x_3 \qquad & = 2, \\
        x_1 + x_2 \qquad + x_4 & = 4,\\
        x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4 & \geq 0.
    \end{align*}
    
    Given a feasible point of the polyhedron, it is possible to analyze if a direction is feasible by checking the following two conditions:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item $Ad = 0$, and
        \item $d_i \geq 0$ when $x_i = 0$,
    \end{itemize}
    where
    $$A \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*}, x = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{pmatrix*}, d = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] d_1 \\ d_2 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*}.$$
    
    
    Let's verify if these conditions are satisfied for the four cases.
    
    \begin{enumerate}
%%%---part (a)---%%%
        \item $\tilde{x}_a = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$, $\tilde{d}_a = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}.$ \vspace{0.5em} \newline
        We first need to compute the values of the slack variables
        $x_3$ and $x_4$ and we obtain $x_3 = 0$ and $x_4 =
        2$. Therefore, we have
        \[
       x_a = \cvect{0 \\ 2 \\ 0 \\ 2},
       \]
       which is indeed a feasible point. The direction in $\R^4$ is
       \[
       d_a = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 \\ 0 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*}.
       \]
        In order to satisfy the first condition, we must have
        \begin{equation*}
        Ad_a = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*} \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 1 \\ 0 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 + d_3 \\ 1 + d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*},
        \end{equation*}
        which is verified by setting $d_3 = 1$ and $d_4 =
        -1$. Therefore, the direction
        \[
d_a = \rvect{1 \\ 0 \\ 1 \\ -1}
        \]
        satisfies the first condition. For the second condition, we look
        at the entries 1 and 3 of $d_a$, because $(x_a)_1=(x_a)_3=0$
        and the only zero entries. As
        $(d_a)_1=1$ and $(d_a)_3=1$, both entries of the direction
        are non negative and we conclude that the direction is feasible
        at the given point. 
        
%
%
%%%---part (b)---%%%
        \item $\tilde{x}_b = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$, $\tilde{d}_b = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}.$ \vspace{0.5em} \newline
        In this case, it is immediate to see that the direction is not
        feasible, as the second condition is not verified for the
        first entry: $(\tilde{d}_b)_1 = -1 < 0$ and $(\tilde{x}_b)_1 = 0$.
        \vspace{1em}
%
%
%%%---part (c)---%%%        
        \item $\tilde{x}_c = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 2 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$, $\tilde{d}_c = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}.$ \vspace{0.5em} \newline
        We compute the values of the slack variables: $x_3 = 2$, $x_4
        = 0$, so that
        \[
   {x}_c= \cvect{2 \\ 2 \\ 2 \\ 0}, 
   \]
   and the direction is
   \[
{d}_c = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*}.
   \]
   For the first condition to be satisfied, we must have
        \begin{equation*}
        Ad = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*} \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 2 + d_3 \\ 2 + d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*},
        \end{equation*}
        which is verified if $d_3 = -2$ and $d_4 = -2$. Therefore,
        \[
{d}_c = \rvect{0 \\ 2 \\ -2 \\ -2}
\]
satisfies the first condition.
        For the second
        condition, we need to look at the entry 4 of $d_c$, as
        $({x}_c)_4=0$ is the only zero entry of $x_c$. As  $({d}_c)_4 = -2 < 0$, the second condition is not satisfied, and the  direction is not feasible at the given point.
        \vspace{1em}
%
%
%%%---part (d)---%%%     
        \item $\tilde{x}_d = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 3 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*}$, $\tilde{d}_d = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \end{pmatrix*}$ \vspace{0.5em} \newline
        We compute the values of the slack variables: $x_3 = 5$, $x_4
        = 1$, so that
        \[
         {x}_d = \cvect{3 \\ 0 \\ 5 \\ 1},
         \]
         and the direction is
         \[
{d}_d = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*}.
         \]
        For the first condition to be satisfied, we must have
        \begin{equation*}
        A{d}_d = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 & 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix*} \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 2 \\ d_3 \\ d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 2 + d_3 \\ 2 + d_4 \end{pmatrix*} = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 0 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix*},
        \end{equation*}
        from which we obtain $d_3 = -2$ and $d_4 = -2$. Therefore,
        \[
d_d = \rvect{0 \\ 2 \\ -2 \\ -2}
\]
verifies the first condition.
For the second condition, we need to look at the entry 2 of $d$, as
$x_2= 0$ is the only zero entry of $x$. As  $d_2 = 2 \geq 0$, the second condition is satisfied, and  the  direction is feasible at the given point.
    \end{enumerate}
