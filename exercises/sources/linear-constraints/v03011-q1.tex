

Consider the following polyhedron represented in canonical form:
\[
 \mathcal{P} = \{x \in \R^2 \mid Ax \leq b\},
\]
with
\[
 A = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] -1 &  1 \\  1 &  1 \\ -1 &  0 \\  0 & -1 \end{pmatrix*}, \qquad b = \begin{pmatrix*}[r] 2 \\ 4 \\ 0 \\ 0  \end{pmatrix*}. 
\]

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Write explicitly the inequality constraints.
    \item Draw the polyhedron.
    \item Write the polyhedron in standard form.
\end{enumerate}
