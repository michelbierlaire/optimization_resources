Consider the following linear optimization problem:
\begin{align*}
 & \min_{x \in \R} 2 x_1 + x_2 \\
 \text{subject to} & \\
 & x_1 + x_2 = 3, \\
 & x_1 \geq 0, \\
 & x_2 \geq 0.
\end{align*}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Does the solution $x_1^* = 0$, $x_2^* = 3$ verify all the constraints of the problem? Which constraints are active at $(x_1^*, x_2^*)$?
    \item Answer the same questions with $x_1^* = 2$, $x_2^* = 2$.
\end{enumerate}
