Consider the optimization problem
\[
\min x_1
\]
subject to
\begin{align*}
x_1+x_2+x_3 & =1,\\
-x_1+2x_2+x_4 &=2,\\
x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4 & \geq 0.
\end{align*}
Consider the basis where the  variables
$x_1$ and $x_2$ are in the basis, and calculate all reduced costs for
that basis. Remember that the reduced cost associated with a non basic variable $x_p$ is defined as
\[
\bar{c}_p = c_p - c_B^T B^{-1} A_p,
\]
where $c_B$ are the entries of the vector $c$ correpsonding to the basic variables, and $A_p$ is the $p$th column of $A$.
