Consider the polyhedron defined by the following constraints:
\begin{align*}
x_1+x_2 &\leq 1,\\
-x_1+2x_2 &\leq 2,\\
x_1,x_2 & \geq 0.
\end{align*}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Draw the polyhedron.
\item Write the polyhedron in standard form.
\item If there are degenerate vertices, identify them on the graphical
  representation and provide the corresponding values of the
  variables.
\item  Consider the case where the  variables  $x_1$ and $x_2$ are in the basis.
Calculate all basic directions, determine if they are feasible of not, and
represent them on the picture.
\end{enumerate}
