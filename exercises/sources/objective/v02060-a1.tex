If $f: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ is a differentiable
function, the function $\nabla f(x): \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^n$ is called the gradient of $f$ and is defined as 
\[
  \nabla f(x_1,x_2,x_3)
=
\cvect{
     \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1} \\*[1.2ex]
    \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}  \\*[1.2ex]
    \frac{\partial f}{\partial x_3} 
}.
\]
Therefore, the gradient of the function is
\[
 \nabla f(x_1,x_2,x_3)
=
\cvect{
     e^{x_1}+2x_1x_3-x_2x_3 \\
    -x_1x_3 \\
    x_1^2-x_1x_2
}.
\]
 Let $f: \mathbb{R}^n \rightarrow \mathbb{R}$ be a differentiable
 function. Consider $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and $d \in \mathbb{R}^n$. The
 directional derivative of $f$ at $x$ in the direction $d$ is given by
 \[
\lim_{\alpha\to 0} \frac{f(x+ \alpha d)-f(x)}{\alpha}, 
\]
if the limit exists. Here, we have
\begin{align*}
f(x+ \alpha d)-f(x) =& e^{x_1+\alpha d_1} + (x_1+\alpha
d_1)^2(x_3+\alpha d_3) \\ & -(x_1+\alpha d_1)(x_2+\alpha d_2)(x_3+\alpha
d_3) \\&- (e^{x_1} + x_1^2x_3 -x_1x_2x_3) \\
=& e^{x_1+\alpha d_1}- e^{x_1} + \alpha x_1^2 d_3 + \alpha^2 x_3
d_1^2+ \alpha^3 d_1^2 d_3 + 2\alpha x_1 x_3 d_1 \\&+ 2 \alpha^2x_1 d_1
d_3 -\alpha x_1 x_2 d_3 - \alpha x_1 x_3 d_2 - \alpha^2x_1 d_2 d_3 \\&-
\alpha x_2 x_3 d_1 - \alpha^2 x_2 d_1 d_3 -\alpha^2 x_3 d_1 d_2 -
\alpha^3 d_1 d_2 d_3.
\end{align*}
Therefore,
 \begin{align*}
\lim_{\alpha\to 0} \frac{f(x+ \alpha d)-f(x)}{\alpha} =& \lim_{\alpha\to 0}\frac{e^{x_1+\alpha d_1}- e^{x_1}}{\alpha} \\&+  x_1^2 d_3 + 2 x_1 x_3 d_1  - x_1 x_2 d_3 -  x_1 x_3 d_2 -
x_2 x_3 d_1 \\
=&d_1 e^{x_1}+  x_1^2 d_3 + 2 x_1 x_3 d_1  - x_1 x_2 d_3 -  x_1 x_3
d_2 -x_2 x_3 d_1 \\
=&d_1 (e^{x_1}+ 2 x_1 x_3 -x_2 x_3 )-  x_1 x_3
d_2  +  (x_1^2  - x_1 x_2) d_3.
 \end{align*}



In addition, when the gradient exists, the
directional derivative is the inner product between the gradient of
$f$ and the direction $d$, that is,
\[
\nabla f(x)^Td.
\]
Using this formula, the directional derivative is
\begin{align*}
(d_1 \, d_2 \,d_3)\nabla f(x_1,x_2,x_3)=
\end{align*}
\[d_1(e^{x_1}+2x_1x_3-x_2x_3)-d_2x_1x_3+d_3(x_1^2-x_1x_2). \]
You can verify that it is the same result as above.
