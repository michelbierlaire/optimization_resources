
The steepest descent direction is the direction opposite to the
gradient. We have
$$\nabla f(x)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_1}\\*[1.2ex]
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x_2}
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
x_1\\
4x_2
\end{pmatrix}.$$

At point $x=(1 \, 1)^T$, we have
\[
\nabla f(x)
=
\begin{pmatrix}
x_1\\
4x_2
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
4
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Therefore, the steepest descent direction is
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-1\\
-4
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The directional derivative is
\[
-\nabla f(x)^T \nabla f(x)= \begin{pmatrix}
-1 & -4
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
4
\end{pmatrix}
=
-17.
\]
