
Consider the following quadratic function:
\begin{equation}
f(x_1,x_2)=2x_1^2+9x_2^2.
\end{equation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Calculate the condition number of the Hessian of $f$  at point $(x_1,x_2)$. 
\item Apply the change of variable $x'= M x$ where
  \[
  M= \begin{pmatrix}
    2 & 0 \\ 0 & 3 \sqrt{2}
\end{pmatrix},
  \]
  that is,
  \begin{align*}
    x'_1 &= 2 x_1, \\
    x'_2 &= 3 \sqrt{2} x_2.
  \end{align*}
  Consider the function
  \[
\tilde{f}(x') = f(M^{-1}x').
\]
Calculate the condition number of the Hessian (using the 2-norm) of $\tilde{f}$ at point $(x'_1,x'_2)$. 
\end{enumerate}
\vspace{3cm}
Remember that,
if $A \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ is a non singular symmetric matrix, then the condition number of $A$ is
\begin{equation}
\kappa(A)=||A||\,||A^{-1}||.
\end{equation}
The norm 2 of a matrix is its largest singular value. Therefore, in this case,
\begin{equation}
\kappa_2(A)=||A||_2||A^{-1}||_2=\frac{\sigma_1}{\sigma_n},
\end{equation}
where $\sigma_1$ is the largest singular value of $A$ and $\sigma_n$ is the smallest. By extension, the condition number of a singular matrix (i.e., such that $\sigma_n=0$) is $+\infty$. If $A$ is symmetric positive semidefinite, the singular values of $A$ are its eigenvalues.
