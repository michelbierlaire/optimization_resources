
We have
\begin{align*}
  f(x) &= \frac{x^2}{100} -2x + 1,\\
  f'(x) &= \frac{2x}{100}-2,\\
  f''(x) &= \frac{2}{100}.
\end{align*}
We need to find $M$ such that
\[
|f'(x)-f'(y)| \leq M |x-y|.
\]
We have
\[
|f'(x)-f'(y)| = \left|\frac{2x}{100}-\frac{2y}{100}\right| = \frac{2}{100}|x-y|.
\]
Therefore, any $M$ such that
\[
M \geq \frac{2}{100}
\]
is a Lipschitz constant.
