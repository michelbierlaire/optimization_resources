
Consider the optimization problem

$$\min_{x \in \mathbb{R}^2} x_1+4x_2$$ subject to 
\begin{align*}
x_1+x_2 & = 2,\\
x_1 & \geq 0,\\
x_2 & \geq 0.
\end{align*}

for which the solution is $x^*=(2,0)^T$ with an optimal value $2$.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Write a new optimization problem by relaxing the constraint
  $2-x_1-x_2=0$, and penalizing its violation with a parameter $\lambda$.
\item Solve the new problem for $\lambda
  = 0$, $1$ and $2$, and, for each case, discuss about the value of the
  objective function at the optimal solution. 
\end{enumerate}
