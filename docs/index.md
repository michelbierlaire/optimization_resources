# Teaching Optimization

This website provides a structured collection of teaching material for optimization, including videos, slides, exercises, notebooks, and a reference textbook.

The content is organized along three complementary dimensions:

- **Topics**: the conceptual structure of optimization (formulation, simplex, duality, etc.)
- **Tracks**: the teaching context in which the material is used
  - *Online course*: video-based material and supporting resources
  - *EPFL course*: lecture slides, exercises, and notebooks used in class
- **Resource types**: textbook, slides, videos, exercises, and notebooks

Depending on your needs, you can explore the material in different ways.

## How to navigate

- Browse by **topic** to study a specific concept
- Browse by **track** to follow a coherent course (online or EPFL)
- Browse by **resource type** to access a specific format

Some topics are covered by multiple resources that are similar but not identical, as they were developed for different teaching contexts. The **Tracks** section helps you identify and follow the material that best matches your use case.
